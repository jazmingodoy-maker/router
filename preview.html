PURPOSE: A lifestyle campaign image for a premium homewares brand — quiet, editorial, tactile. Designed to lead a hero banner or editorial spread for a seasonal collection launch. The image should feel curated, not constructed. MOOD: Quiet, intimate, slow-living. Emotionally restrained with a sense of warmth. Not aspirational in a loud way — more like a found moment, personal and unhurried. The mood of a Sunday morning before the world wakes up. AUDIENCE / BRAND FEEL: Design-literate adults aged 28–45 who value craft, provenance, and considered aesthetics. The brand occupies the space between high-street accessible and luxury — premium without ostentation. Familiar with editorial style references like Kinfolk, Cereal, and Apartamento. SUBJECT / SCENE: A lifestyle photograph centred on a single handmade ceramic vessel placed on a linen surface near a window. Morning light falls softly across the scene. A dried botanical element rests nearby. No hands, no people — the object speaks for itself. Subtle surface textures are foregrounded. COMPOSITION / LAYOUT: Object-centred with generous breathing room. Soft asymmetry — the vessel placed slightly off-centre to the left. Negative space is intentional and active. Portrait-oriented with space at the top for a headline overlay. Low horizon, intimate perspective. COPY / TEXT: Headline: "Made slowly. Kept long." — set in a refined serif, small scale, positioned in the upper third. A secondary metadata label reads "Collection No. 4 — Spring". No further body copy in this frame. TYPOGRAPHY: Small editorial type. Refined serif accents for the headline. Metadata-style labelling for secondary information. All type restrained in size and weight — never competing with the image. Generous tracking on label text. COLOUR: Warm neutrals throughout. Linen, greige, warm off-white, muted earthy tones. Low saturation — no bright accents. The ceramic may carry a soft blush or sage glaze that reads quietly within the neutral field. Warm light temperature throughout. AVOID: Busy backgrounds, pattern-heavy surfaces, high saturation, bright or cold lighting, lifestyle clichés (coffee cups, laptops, flat-lays with too many objects), overly styled scenes, stock-photograph feel, visible branding or logos, heavy drop shadows, modern sans-serif type at large scale.
